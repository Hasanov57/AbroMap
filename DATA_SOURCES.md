# Data sources and trust rules

| Data | Source | Use and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field hierarchy | [UNESCO ISCED-F 2013](https://www.uis.unesco.org/en/methods-and-tools/isced), with the [classification appendix](https://www.statistik.at/kdb/downloads/pdf/prod/IscedFieldsOfEducationTraining2013_Manual.pdf) | 11 broad, 29 narrow and 80 detailed fields. Codes are official; 260 additional specific major labels are an AbroMap-curated search layer, not UNESCO codes. |
| University identity and metadata | [ROR v2 data dump](https://ror.readme.io/docs/data-dump), [release DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23500708) | ROR metadata is CC0 1.0. The bundled 2026-10-20 release has a verified MD5 checksum in `backend/data/ror_snapshot_manifest.json`. Selection accepts active education records with a university/equivalent name cue in supported countries. This conservative filter can omit valid institutions. ROR does not verify degree programs or admission requirements. |
| Country display names | Unicode CLDR via `Intl.DisplayNames`; supported ISO codes curated for this product | 45 European countries, five transcontinental countries, China and the United States. Country availability is a catalog setting, not a claim that programs have been verified there. |
| Program and admission facts | [TUM Informatics MSc](https://www.cit.tum.de/en/cit/studies/degree-programs/master-informatics/), [Politecnico di Milano Computer Science and Engineering](https://www.polimi.it/en/education/laurea-magistrale-programmes/programme-detail/computer-science-and-engineering-ingegneria-informatica), [ELTE Computer Science MSc](https://www.elte.hu/en/computer-science-msc), [Tsinghua Master in Management 2027](https://masters.sem.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1007/2172.htm) | Four source-checked master's programs, reviewed 2026-10-28. Only explicit facts were entered. Unknown tuition, GPA, IELTS and deadlines remain blank. TUM and Polimi pages used here do not establish an intake-specific year, so their requirements are marked `year_unverified`. ELTE and Tsinghua use their stated 2027 intake. Recheck official pages before applying. |
| Scholarship scheme | [Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters) | A scheme-level listing, not an award guarantee. Eligibility, deadline and available funding depend on each consortium. |

English, Azerbaijani and Russian labels and aliases are available for broad and common fields. The full 260-major catalog has English labels; many specific majors still need reviewed Azerbaijani and Russian translations. User-entered custom majors are saved as text and do not produce a match until mapped to the taxonomy.

ROR identity is keyed by exact ROR ID. Same-name records with different IDs are held in `university_review_candidates`; they are not automatically merged. Program facts are keyed by official URL and have source publisher, review date and requirement status. Matching uses these facts as a compatibility screen, never as an admission probability. A related subject is not proof that a prior degree meets a university's prerequisites.
